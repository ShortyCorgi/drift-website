When you write

For a technical issue, it helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version, Drift version, and a short description of what happened. Only include details you are comfortable sharing.

Please do not send journal entries, passwords, passcodes, authentication codes, or other sensitive information. If you attach a screenshot, check it for private writing first.

Privacy questions

You can use the same email address for privacy requests, including questions about support correspondence. The privacy policy explains how the app and website handle information.

A note about wellbeing

Drift is a calming app, not medical care or an emergency service. Support is not monitored for emergencies. If you need urgent help, contact your local emergency services or someone you trust.
