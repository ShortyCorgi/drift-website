A clear look at what stays on your device, what you control, and what happens when you contact us.

Updated 29 October 2026

A private place to pause

Drift's current version is an offline calming app with an optional Five Lines journal. There is no Drift account, advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or Drift-operated server receiving your writing. Scenes, music, and quotations are bundled with the app.

What stays on your iPhone

Your journal, dates, chosen scene memories, and journal settings are kept in the app's local storage. A protected local recovery copy of an unsaved draft may be kept so a failed save does not silently discard it. App preferences such as sound, motion, favourites, and optional reflection settings use app-only preferences storage, not a private-writing profile. Device storage and operating-system protections apply; journal lock is an access screen, not a promise of separate end-to-end encryption.

Optional personal reflections

Quote matching is off by default. If you enable it, up to fourteen recent pages, limited to 2,000 characters, may be processed by Apple's on-device language model to choose one broad theme. If that model is unavailable, simple local word matching is used. Only a sourced quotation is displayed; Drift does not generate diagnoses or advice. Drift does not send writing to an external AI service, use it for model training, or add it to its own logs. Theme results are kept only in memory. Selected journal memories are also off by default and use only pages you explicitly approve. Both features pause when journal locking is enabled.

Journal lock and app switching

Optional Face ID, Touch ID, or device-passcode authentication is handled by iOS. Drift does not receive or store your biometric data or passcode. When journal lock is enabled, leaving the app hides journal content and returning requires authentication before reading it again. Keep your device and backups secure; no app can protect data on a compromised device.

Reminders and permissions

A daily journal reminder is optional. Notification permission is requested only when you turn the reminder on. Reminders are scheduled locally and contain a general invitation, not journal text. Denying permission does not prevent journaling.

Exports and external links

Drift exports writing only when you choose to share it. Markdown and JSON export files contain private journal text; any destination or recipient you choose receives that copy. Deleting the original journal cannot delete exported copies or copies in backups. Opening a quotation source or the public privacy-policy link visits an external website, whose own privacy practices apply. Drift does not attach journal text to those links.

Backups, retention, and deletion

There is no app-managed cloud synchronisation. Local journal data and preferences may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, depending on your Apple settings. Recovery drafts are excluded from device backup and normally cleared after a confirmed save, an explicit recovery-draft discard, or journal deletion. Your journal remains locally until you delete it or remove the app. Journal deletion removes the saved entries and cancels the reminder, then attempts to remove recovery copies and app-owned temporary exports. If that file cleanup fails, Drift shows a warning and offers retry; a local copy may remain until cleanup succeeds. Device backups and copies already shared elsewhere remain under your control.

Your choices

You can keep journaling entirely separate from quotes and scene memories, turn either reflection option off in Settings, change which pages may appear, export your writing, or delete the journal. No account deletion is needed because Drift does not create accounts. Apple's own optional device diagnostics and backup services are governed by your Apple settings and Apple's policies.

The public Drift website

The Drift website has no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, contact forms, or cookies set by the site. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records website visitors' IP addresses for security purposes under its own privacy statement, linked below. Visiting the website is separate from using the offline app; journal text is not attached to website links.

When you contact support

If you choose to email Drift's developer, the developer receives your email address and the information you include through the email provider, Gmail. That correspondence is used to respond to support and privacy requests. Please avoid sending journal entries, passwords, passcodes, or authentication codes. Support messages are separate from the local journal and are retained in the email account as needed to handle the request; you can ask about access, correction, or deletion using the contact address below. Removing the app or deleting a journal does not delete an email you have sent.

Policy updates and contact

This policy describes the current local-first app and its public informational website. Material changes to data handling must be reflected here and in the published policy before release. For privacy or support questions, contact Drift's developer at the email address listed below.
